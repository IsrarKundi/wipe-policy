Privacy Policy — Wipe: Photo Cleaner & Gallery

Developer: Artivo
Contact: israrkundi37@gmail.com
Effective date: 20 November 2026

Overview

Wipe is a photo-cleaning app for Android. It helps you review your gallery, keep the photos you want, and delete the rest. This policy explains what the app does with your photos and your information. In short: your photos stay on your device. Wipe collects nothing about your photos. The only information that ever leaves your device is purchase information for the optional Wipe Pro subscription, as described under Wipe Pro purchases below.

What Wipe does NOT collect

  • No account, no sign-in. Wipe has no accounts, no login, and no user profiles.
  • No photos leave your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, backed up, or sent to any server by Wipe.
  • No analytics, no tracking. Wipe contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software.
  • No third-party sharing, except for purchases. Wipe shares no data with any third party, except for subscription purchases — see Wipe Pro purchases below.

What Wipe processes on your device

To do its job, Wipe accesses the photos and videos already stored on your device, and only while you use the app:

  • Clean deck: shows your photos so you can swipe to keep or delete them.
  • Albums: groups your photos (by month, and into smart groups such as Duplicates, Old photos, Videos, Large photos, Blurry photos, and Dark photos) so you can review them faster.
  • Duplicates: groups photos that look alike and lets you pick one photo to keep.
  • Trash: photos you delete from the deck wait here for 30 days, so you can restore them. After 30 days they are removed.
  • Review: photos you mark here and delete are deleted permanently. They do not go to Trash.

All of this processing happens on your device. Deleting a photo in Wipe uses your device's own delete function, which may ask you to confirm the deletion.

Wipe Pro purchases

Wipe Pro is an optional paid subscription that unlocks smart finders for duplicates, blurry photos, and dark photos. Everything else in Wipe stays free, and you can use Wipe without subscribing.

  • Payments are handled by Google Play. When you subscribe, Google Play processes your payment. Wipe never sees your payment details.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions. To check whether your subscription is active, Wipe uses RevenueCat. When you subscribe, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt. It does not receive your photos, your name, or your email address.
  • No account. Wipe Pro does not need an account, and subscribing does not create one.

Permissions

Wipe asks for access to your photos and media. This permission is needed to show, organise, and delete photos at your request. Wipe uses this permission for nothing else, and it does not access your photos when the app is not in use.

Data security

Your photos remain under your device's own security (lock screen, encryption, and permissions) at all times. Purchase information is handled by Google Play and RevenueCat as described above; Wipe stores no purchase data on its own servers, because Wipe has none.

Children

Wipe is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date. Because Wipe collects no data about your photos, a policy change cannot retroactively change how your photos were handled.
